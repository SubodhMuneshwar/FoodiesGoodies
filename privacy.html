Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2025

At Foodies Goodies, we value your trust and are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, store, and utilize information when you use our social recipe platform, AI Dietician calculators, and culinary exploration services.

1. What Data We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly to us when creating an account, interacting with the community, or contacting support:

  • Account Information: Name, username, email address, and encrypted credentials securely stored via password hashing algorithms.
  • Culinary Profile Data: Bio, dietary focus (e.g. Mediterranean, Vegan, Keto, High-Protein), kitchen rank, and profile avatars.
  • User-Generated Content: Recipes you create, ingredients, preparation steps, food photographs, flavor notes, and comments.
  • AI Dietician Inputs: Age, weight, height, activity level, and dietary targets entered for nutritional calculations (processed in-session).

2. How We Use Your Information

We utilize the collected information for the following core purposes:

  • Operating and enhancing the Foodies Goodies platform, kitchen dashboards, and social community feeds.
  • Delivering accurate nutrition calculations, macro distributions, and recipe discovery.
  • Authenticating your session and preventing fraudulent activity, CSRF attacks, or unauthorized access.
  • Responding promptly to customer inquiries submitted through our contact form.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

Foodies Goodies uses essential cookies and browser LocalStorage to preserve your logged-in session, remember your saved recipes, and cache your preferred dietary search filters. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or sell your personal browsing habits to data brokers.

4. Third-Party Services and APIs

To provide high-quality culinary discovery, we interact with select third-party services:

  • Edamam Recipe API: Recipe search queries are routed through our secure server-side proxy to Edamam for live recipe and nutrition lookup. No personal user credentials are shared with Edamam.
  • Unsplash Images: High-resolution food photography assets may be retrieved from Unsplash CDN.

5. Data Retention and Deletion Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

You retain full ownership over your account data. Under GDPR and CCPA guidelines, you have the right to inspect, update, or request permanent deletion of your account and all associated recipes and comments. To exercise these rights, please submit a request through our Contact Us page.

6. Contact Information

If you have questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or our data management practices, please reach out via email at admin@foodiesgoodies.local or through our online contact form.
